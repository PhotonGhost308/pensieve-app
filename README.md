<h1 align="center">Pensieve</h1>

<p align="center"><b>An AI research notebook that keeps up with you.</b><br>
Speak your thoughts, plan your day, read and translate papers, work with Codex — on your computer and your phone, in sync.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://photonghost308.github.io/pensieve-app/"><b>Website & downloads</b></a> · <a href="https://github.com/PhotonGhost308/pensieve-app/releases/latest">Latest release</a></p>

![Pensieve](screenshots/today.webp)

This repository holds Pensieve's website and its downloads for **Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Linux and Android**. The iOS app is coming.

**Install** — download for your system from the [website](https://photonghost308.github.io/pensieve-app/#download) or the [latest release](https://github.com/PhotonGhost308/pensieve-app/releases/latest), install it on the computer you keep running (it is the hub your phone syncs with), add an AI model key in Settings → AI models, and pair your phone in Settings → Sync.

Questions and bug reports: [issues](https://github.com/PhotonGhost308/pensieve-app/issues).
